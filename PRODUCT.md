# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Freelance clients** — founders, communities (e.g. Minecraft servers, Discord groups), agencies and businesses looking for someone to design and build a website or app.
- **Employers / recruiters** — companies evaluating Lakshya for a role.

Both arrive curious, browse the board on desktop or phone, and should leave knowing who he is, what he has shipped, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Lakshya Kumar (Antinity) presented as an interactive investigation board. Success = a visitor understands the person and the work, then emails or DMs him (work enquiry or job conversation).

## Positioning

The portfolio itself is the proof of craft: a cinematic, game-quality WebGL experience built by the subject, rather than a template.

## Operating Context

- Explored with mouse (torch follows the pointer) or touch; pages are picked up and read like game notes.
- Evaluated quickly by busy people: the identity, the work list and the contact route must be findable without instructions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 (App Router) + React Three Fiber / three.js + postprocessing; content in `src/data/content.js`, layout in `src/data/boardLayout.js`.
- Must stay performant on ordinary laptops (`?fx=low|off` fallbacks) and usable on phones.
- Social links still to be supplied: Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp (placeholders, marked TODO in content).
- Screenshots exist only for Nexora Visuals, Minemen Tier Tests, Minemen Tier List, Unipower, Rural Advertising & Marketing; other works need placeholders until images are provided.

## Product Principles

1. The person first: identity is the centre of the board and the first thing seen.
2. Real facts only; every string and piece of evidence connects things that are actually related.
3. Contact is always one obvious step away.
4. Atmosphere serves legibility, never the other way round.
